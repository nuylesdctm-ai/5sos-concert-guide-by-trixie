🚕 ARRIVING AT THE VENUE

  • Plan your route ahead of time and allow extra travel time.
  • If using a ride-hailing service, confirm your drop-off location with your driver.
  • Follow the event's official traffic and drop-off instructions.
  • Expect possible traffic and queues around the arena.

🌙 GOING HOME AFTER THE SHOW

  • Expect heavy traffic and longer waiting times after the concert.
  • Agree on a pickup point with your companions before the show ends.
  • Confirm your pickup location with your driver.
  • Keep your phone charged while waiting for your ride.
  • Stay in well-lit, designated waiting areas.

Transportation routes, traffic conditions, and pickup arrangements may change. Always check current advisories and follow venue personnel's instructions.

🎟️ TICKET & ENTRY GUIDE

A few helpful reminders to make your entry experience smoother on concert day.

📱 BEFORE TICKET SCANNING

  • Open your official ticket before reaching the entrance.
  • Make sure your ticket QR code is clearly visible on your screen.
  • Increase your screen brightness if needed.
  • Keep your ticket QR code private. Avoid posting or sharing screenshots of it online.
  • Prepare any required identification according to your ticket and event instructions.

🚪 AT THE ENTRANCE

  • Follow the designated entrance and queue instructions.
  • Keep your ticket ready while waiting in line.
  • Follow instructions from security personnel and event marshals.
  • Do not share or transfer your ticket unless permitted by the ticket provider.

⚠️ HAVING TICKET PROBLEMS?

  • If your QR code won't scan, stay calm and ask the authorized ticketing staff for assistance.
  • If you cannot access your ticket, check your ticket provider's official app or website.
  • Do not rely on screenshots or forwarded tickets as proof of admission unless the ticket provider allows them.
  • Never post your ticket barcode or QR code in public groups.

Gate assignments, entry procedures, and ticket requirements may vary. Follow the official 5SOS Manila event announcements and your ticket provider's instructions.

💜 CONCERT DAY SURVIVAL TIPS

Make the most of concert day while keeping yourself and the people around you safe.

👟 STAY COMFORTABLE

  • Wear comfortable shoes suitable for standing and walking.
  • Consider bringing earplugs if you're sensitive to loud sounds.
  • Eat properly before the show.
  • Stay hydrated and take care of yourself.

📱 KEEP YOUR BELONGINGS SAFE

  • Keep your phone, wallet, and valuables secure.
  • Use a bag that you can comfortably keep close to you.
  • Keep your phone charged for communication and transportation.

🫶 RESPECT THE CROWD

  • Be mindful of other fans' personal space.
  • Avoid pushing, shoving, or blocking other people's view.
  • Be considerate when taking photos or recording videos.
  • Follow instructions from venue staff and event marshals.

🩺 LISTEN TO YOUR BODY

  • If you feel dizzy, weak, or unwell, seek assistance from venue personnel.
  • Don't hesitate to approach medical staff when needed.
  • Never push yourself to stay in a crowded area if you feel unsafe.

Your safety matters just as much as enjoying the show. Look out for yourself and your fellow fans!

👥If separated from friendsAgree on a meeting point before the concert starts.

🚪Know your nearest exitTake note of designated exits and follow venue staff instructions.
⚠️During an evacuationDo not use elevators unless instructed by authorities. Follow official evacuation directions.
